# snow-countdown-calendar

A countdown calendar with a live timer — tap a day and draw an ✕ to cross it off. 🗓️

Meet the mascot who cheers you on as the days go by.

This project was made for a dear friend to encourage her and remind her that she's doing a great job, one day at a time. 💙

## Meet the mascots

| Mascot        | Who                                   | Celebrates with                       |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Snow** ☃️   | A snowman in a knitted hat and scarf  | Falling snowflakes ❄️                 |
| **Bean** 🎤   | A little duck with a microphone       | A concert of notes and instruments 🎶 |
| **Teddy** 🥁  | A bear in a marching-band uniform     | Bouncing drums and honey 🍯           |
| **Coffee** 🐱 | An orange cat with a leaf on its head | Drifting leaves and coffee cups 🍃    |

Cross off 10 days and your mascot gets a new accessory — six to collect in all.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no frameworks, no dependencies. The mascots, stickers and accessories are all inline SVG, and the share cards are drawn on a `<canvas>`.

## Your data

Your countdown, crossed-off days and settings are kept in your browser's `localStorage`. Nothing is sent anywhere, so clearing your site data (or switching browsers) starts a fresh calendar.
